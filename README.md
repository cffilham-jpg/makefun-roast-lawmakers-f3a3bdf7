## Entry

> The lawmaker who wrote Section 12 of the National AI Safety Act — 'all models must be auditable by design' — just submitted his own 312-page crypto bill as a screenshot because the Export button was, in his words, 'hiding from him'.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the entry sentence to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `out`), as UTF-8 bytes with no trailing newline, then validates the output. Run it with `python3 main.py`.
